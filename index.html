    Why it matters

    Knowing each other is never finished.

    Most couples feel they know each other well, until a simple question catches them out. Real closeness goes beyond favourite foods and birthdays. It's knowing what your partner is quietly worried about, what they're proud of, and what they still dream about.

    People change over the years, and so do their answers. That's why staying curious matters more than having every answer right. A small, honest question asked often does more for a relationship than a big gesture made once a year.

    A few questions to start with

    • If money weren't a worry, how would you spend your days?
    • Which place from your childhood still feels like home?
    • What's one thing you wish people asked you about more often?
    • Which family tradition would you never let go of?
